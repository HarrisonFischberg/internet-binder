1. What it is: Internet Binder for Mac is a Safari web extension, delivered in a small Mac app. It is the Mac version of the Internet Binder iPhone and iPad app (same App Store record, universal purchase). It is a personal bookmarking app for people who save things they find online (recipes, places, products, workouts, videos, articles) and lose track of them. It sorts each save by what it is and helps put it to use: cooking mode for recipes, places grouped by city, goals for workouts.

2. Setup: No account, no sign-in, no login credentials and no sample files needed. Open Internet Binder once and click Open Safari Settings. In Safari Settings > Extensions, tick Internet Binder, then allow it on websites when Safari asks (or choose Always Allow on Every Website). Then open any recipe or shop page in Safari and click the Internet Binder button in the toolbar, or right-click the page, a link or a picture and choose Save to Internet Binder. Click the toolbar button again and choose Open dashboard to see the library, with Review, recipes, places and search. There are no user accounts, so there is no registration or account deletion flow. Nothing is shared with other people, and there is no paid content or in-app purchase.

3. External services: Apple iCloud (optional sync, in the user's own private iCloud). Optional, user's own API key, off by default: Anthropic or OpenAI, for users who want AI to tidy titles and fill in details; the app works fully without it. Optional lookups, off by default, fill in missing public details from OpenStreetMap (places), Wikidata (films and shows) and Open Library (books). Page titles and pictures are read from the page the user chooses to save. No analytics, ads or tracking.

4. Regions: The app works the same in all regions, with no region-specific content or features.

5. Regulated industries / third-party material: Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry and includes no protected third-party content; users only save links to pages they choose.
