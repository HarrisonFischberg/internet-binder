## What Keepsake is
A private place to keep good finds from the web and your phone: products, places, recipes, workouts,
videos, articles, films and shows, books. Keepsake recognises what each save is, files it, and gives
each kind its own useful view (cook a recipe, get directions to a place, check in a workout).

## Who it is for
People who save a lot and lose track of it: screenshots, open tabs, Instagram saves, notes to self.
They want one place, sorting done for them, and a calm UI. No account, no feed, nothing social.

## Platforms
- Chrome extension 1.4.4 (live): toolbar popup, on-page save buttons and toast, dashboard.
- Safari for Mac: the same web extension in a small Mac container app (built, not submitted).
- iPhone and iPad app (release candidate): Home, Library, Places, Review, kind views, share sheet, widgets.
- Planned: Mac app and iCloud sync (M4).

## Principles
1. Saved first, sorted second: capture never blocks on a question.
2. One thing in front per screen; the rest steps back.
3. The save is the hero, not the chrome.
4. Private by default and says so once, plainly.
5. Same names and same look on every device.

## Voice
Plain, warm, short. Sentence case. No em dashes, no jargon ("item", "queue", "signals").
