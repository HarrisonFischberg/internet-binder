Field Notebook: one warm, writerly notebook across iPhone and Chrome, with graph paper, serif names, mono labels and a single highlighter swipe for what matters.

# Option 1 · Field Notebook

**Rationale.** Keepsake is a place where you jot down good finds to come back to, and a naturalist's field notebook is exactly that object: calm, personal, a bit handmade, and built for lists rather than feeds. This direction replaces both current looks (cool Modern on Chrome, Postcard on iOS) with one identity: graph-paper ground, graphite ink, Newsreader-style serif for names, small uppercase mono labels for facts ("RECIPE · 6 INGREDIENTS"), one highlighter yellow used only to mark the thing that matters right now (the active nav item, the best-guess collection, the Review nudge), and red pencil reserved for stamps such as Cooked and Been and for place labels. Saves become taped photos or ruled index cards, so a missing picture looks deliberate (the title hand-lettered on a card) instead of a giant pastel icon. It is the quietest of the five and the closest to Keepsake's "calm, private library" promise.

**Key changes by platform**
- **iPhone:** Home becomes a short notebook page: dated header, dashed Review nudge with the highlighter swipe, a taped-photo Recent strip, ruled rows for Collections and Lifestyle, and "Come back to" as ruled index rows. Library is a ruled list (photo or index card, mono fact line, serif title, italic note) with kind chips and an inline search field; a Search tab replaces the floating search dock. The tab bar is a solid paper strip, not glass over content. Recipe puts Start cooking in one solid bottom bar with Share beside it; the Cooked stamp moves up beside the title, so nothing floats over the ingredients and the tab bar is hidden on this screen. Review is a stack of index cards: best-guess collection highlighted, two alternatives, then "Somewhere else…" and "Skip for now" as quiet text.
- **iPad:** same sidebar as the Chrome dashboard (paper sidebar, highlighter on the active row, mono section labels), list and detail panes as ruled pages; recipe detail uses the two-column ingredients and steps layout from Chrome.
- **Chrome / Safari extension popup:** "Saved to Home Decor ▾" with a highlighter check, taped photo, mono kind line, serif title, price, an inline "Add a note…" line, Undo plus one primary "Open in Keepsake". Footer in mono: "Saved on this device".
- **Chrome / Safari dashboard:** paper sidebar with Kinds and Collections sections and counts in mono; Library is a five-column grid of taped photos and index cards; a save opens a reading view (photo, Cooked stamp, note, serif title, Start cooking primary, Mark cooked secondary, Edit and Share quiet; ingredients and numbered steps side by side) instead of the "Item details" edit form; Review rows show the best guess highlighted with 1/2/3 key hints, a dashed "Somewhere else…" chip, and Skip/Open as quiet text, with Delete moved into Open.
- **Safari Mac app shell:** the setup window becomes a single notebook page: serif "Keepsake", a mono step list ("1 · Open Safari Settings · 2 · Turn on Keepsake"), one dark primary button.

**What stays the same:** the information architecture (Home, Library, Review, Lifestyle kinds, Collections, Places), every name and count, the one-fact-per-kind rule, the Review learning loop and 1/2/3 keys, Undo after every change, the bookmark mark, "Saved on this device", and the kind icon set (now drawn in ink rather than coloured floods).

**Accessibility:** ink #2B2A27 and soft ink #5E5A52 on paper #F6F2E7 pass AA (about 13:1 and 6.3:1); the highlighter is only ever behind ink text; red pencil #A63A28 is about 6:1 on paper. Dark mode (chalk on #1C1B18) keeps the same roles. Checkbox outlines use the soft ink for 3:1 non-text contrast.

**Fonts (mockup stand-ins → shipped):** Newsreader → New York (system serif) on Apple, Iowan Old Style/Georgia stack on Chrome; IBM Plex Mono → SF Mono on Apple, ui-monospace stack on Chrome; Inter → SF Pro / system-ui. No bundled or CDN fonts needed.
