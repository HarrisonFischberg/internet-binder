# Keepsake 2.2.0

Everything in 2.1.4 (see 2.1.4.md), plus:

## New

- **Each style has its own page background behind the tiles**, in light and dark (iPhone, iPad and the
  Chrome/Safari dashboard). Pantry Pop: warm glows in the jar colours and a printed halftone. Field
  Notebook: graph paper with a heavier line every fifth square and a warm vignette. Night Atlas: an amber
  lamp, a globe grid rising from the corner and stars after dark. Swiss Index: column hairlines and one
  red disc low in the corner. The pattern fades out under the title bars, and Increase Contrast (or
  `prefers-contrast: more`) keeps the page plain. The toolbar popup is unchanged.
- **Review is a card in the share sheet and the toolbar popup**, beside Collections and Lifestyle, with
  a fan of what is already waiting. One tap files the save there.
- **Music while cooking starts by itself** when Cooking mode opens. On silent it plays muted until you
  press a volume button or tap play.

## Fixed

- **iPhone share sheet no longer closes by itself.** On 2.1.4 it could flash and close before you
  picked a home, so the "Saved to …" notification never came. It now stays open until Done or Undo
  (a swipe down doesn't close it), and it reads only a few saves per group, so big libraries stay well
  inside the share extension's memory limit.
- **Appearance → Feel → Sound now ticks.** It used the keyboard-click sound, which iOS mutes when
  Keyboard Clicks is off. It now plays its own soft tock (Silent mode still mutes it), and switching
  it on plays a sample.
- **Music while cooking plays in Silent mode** (it was muted).
