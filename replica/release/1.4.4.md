# Keepsake 1.4.4

1.4.3 was uploaded but its publish was cancelled to update the privacy policy, so 1.4.4 is the first
release after 1.4.2 and includes everything in `1.4.3.md` as well.

## What's new

- **Optional AI understands each save.** With AI on, your own provider works out what every save is (a
  recipe, workout, place, film, book, video, article, product or link) and fills in that kind's card:
  ingredients and steps, exercises with sets, reps and rest, a place's city, state, cuisine and
  category, a film's year, a book's author. It runs after every save and Instagram import (in the
  background, with progress under the header), from **Fix with AI** on any collection or Review, and from
  **Check with AI** in a Lifestyle view for cards that still have gaps.
- What the save already says is kept; the AI fills the gaps. A recipe or routine the post doesn't write
  out (it's only in the video) is drafted and marked **AI draft** on the card. A place's street, phone and
  hours are kept only when the save itself states them. Cards say where their details came from ("Recipe
  read by AI", "Drafted by AI").
- A save moves to a Lifestyle view only when the AI is sure, never when you filed it or picked its kind
  yourself. A kind you pick is read again for that kind's details. Everything is in the change log with
  Undo, and Fix with AI's **Undo all** covers it.
- Fix with AI on a collection like Kitchen now moves the recipes and places in it to Recipes and Places
  with full cards, and then tidies what's left to buy.
- Fixed: an Instagram Saved-collection scan could fail with "Exceeded storage quota" or stop partway (seen
  in Safari). The scan's post list and thumbnails now live in IndexedDB; each thumbnail is sent once on its
  own; the session area keeps a small summary.

## Privacy

- `PRIVACY.md` and https://rarecreatures.app/apps/keepsake/privacy/ (updated 16 October 2026) describe
  what Understanding a save sends: the save's text, Instagram caption, hashtags and location tag, its
  locally stored thumbnail (never a remote image link) and, in the extension, the text of its own page and
  up to two caption links, only for sites already granted, fetched without cookies.

## Data

- New optional item field `understoodAt` (when your provider last read the save), new user field `kind`,
  new recipe/workout `details.drafted` and details sources `ai` / `ai-draft`. Older builds drop them when
  they rewrite a save; nothing to migrate.
- New IndexedDB database `keepsake-scan` for an Instagram scan in progress; cleared after the import.
