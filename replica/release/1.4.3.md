# Keepsake 1.4.3

## What's new

- Fixed: saving an Instagram post or reel could keep no picture and no title. When Instagram names the
  post at `/<user>/reel/<code>/` while you're on `/p/<code>/` and the tab's title is just "Instagram",
  Keepsake took the page's tags for another page's and dropped them. The same post code now counts as
  the same page, so the post's picture and its "Name on Instagram: …" title are kept.
- Workouts on Instagram: a routine listed without an "Ingredients" heading ("10 Upright Rows",
  "10 Horn Curls"…) is a Workout, not a Recipe. A post with two clear workout signs ("routine",
  "#homeworkout", "kettlebell", "reps", "yoga"…) is a Workout even when the routine is only in the video
  (no exercises are made up). Recipes that "set" overnight and a "morning routine" stay what they are.
  The same rules apply to a single save and to Instagram import.
- Instagram spacer lines (".", "…", "⬇️") are no longer read as recipe steps or ingredients, and
  "60 seconds", "1 kettlebell", "10 reps" are not ingredients.
- Workouts read "3–4 rounds" and "Rest for 60s".
- Under the hood: what a page becomes (kind, details, filing) and the after-save fill-in moved from
  `background.js` to `src/shared/capture.js` and `src/shared/fill.js`, unchanged, so the iOS app and the
  Safari version run the same rules.

## Safari

  Store. `tests/safari.test.mjs` checks the Safari copy is this release file for file.
