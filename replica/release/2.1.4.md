# Keepsake 2.1.4

Everything in 2.1.3 (see 2.1.3.md), plus:

## New

- **Select, to move or delete several at once.** A Select button sits at the top of every list of saves
  and of the Collections and Lifestyle pages, beside ✦ Check with AI (Chrome, Safari) or Fix with AI
  (iPhone, iPad). Tick saves, or whole collections and Lifestyle views, then Select all / none, Move to…,
  Favorite or Delete. On the dashboard ⌘A / Ctrl+A selects everything shown, Delete removes the
  selection and Escape leaves Select mode.
- **Long press or right-click any card, row or tile** for its actions without opening it: mark done,
  favorite, Move to…, share, copy link, Fix with AI, Select (starts Select with that one ticked) and
  Delete. Collection tiles offer Pin, Select and Delete (keep its saves, or delete them too).
- **Swipe to delete on iPhone and iPad rows** (Places, Places review, Review): a half swipe shows Delete,
  a full swipe deletes.
- **Undo instead of "Are you sure?".** Every delete, of one save or of many, of a collection or several,
  shows an Undo toast for 8 seconds. Undo puts deleted collections back with every save filed in them
  again.
- **A Map button next to "Places"** switches straight between the list and the map (on iPhone, in
  Lifestyle → Places, which had no map before).

## Changed

- **Exports are about half the size.** Each save's cover picture is written once instead of twice
  (a 1,102-save library went from 76.5 MB to 39.8 MB). Older versions still import the new files.
- **Chrome imports backups up to 150 MB** (was 25 MB).
