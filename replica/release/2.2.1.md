# Patchwork 2.2.1 (formerly Keepsake)

Everything in 2.2.0 (see 2.2.0.md), plus:

## New

- **Keepsake is now Patchwork.** The name changes everywhere people see it: the Chrome Web Store
  listing and toolbar ("Save to Patchwork", Alt+Shift+K "Open Patchwork…"), the right-click menu, the
  dashboard and popup, the in-page save card, the iPhone and iPad home screen, share sheet and widgets,
  and the Safari for Mac app (now Patchwork.app, extension "Patchwork Extension"). Exports are named
  patchwork-export-DATE.json.
- **A new app icon: four pastel scraps, the big one terracotta with a cream bookmark tag.** It is the
  app's own icon on iPhone and iPad (light, dark and tinted), the Mac app, and the Chrome and Safari
  toolbar button (light and dark). At toolbar sizes and the Mac's 16/32 px sizes it sits straight with
  thicker lines so it stays readable.
- The Pantry Pop logo beside the name in the dashboard sidebar and the popup shows the same four
  scraps. The other styles keep their bookmark logo. In the icon pickers the default icon is called
  "Patchwork"; the Field Notebook, Gallery, Night Atlas and Swiss Index icons are unchanged.

## What did not change (so nothing is lost)

- Your library, settings and AI key: storage names, the IndexedDB database and the iOS App Group stay
  the same, so updating keeps everything.
- Store identity: the Chrome extension id, the bundle ids (com.harrisonfischberg.keepsake…), the
  keepsake:// link scheme and the export format marker (`keepsake-export`, so old exports still
  import). Xcode project, scheme and target names and the repo folder names are unchanged too.
- The privacy policy address (rarecreatures.app/apps/keepsake/privacy/) until the website moves.

## Store art

Rebuilt listing icon, promo tiles, screenshots, listing text and the App Store 1024 icon:
`/mnt/project-files/keepsake/store-branding-patchwork/` (tools/ re-renders them).
