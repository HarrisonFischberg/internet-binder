# Internet Binder 2.2.1 (formerly Keepsake)

Everything in 2.2.0 (see 2.2.0.md), plus:

## New

- **Keepsake is now Internet Binder.** The name changes everywhere people see it: the Chrome Web Store
  listing and toolbar ("Save to Internet Binder"), the right-click menu, the dashboard and popup, the in-page
  save card, the iPhone and iPad home screen, share sheet and widgets (shortened to "Binder" there so it fits under the icon), and the Safari for Mac app (now
  Internet Binder.app, extension "Internet Binder Extension"). Exports are named internet-binder-export-DATE.json.

- **A new app icon: a sky-blue binder with terracotta, butter and mint index tabs and a cream bookmark
  tag** (picked Oct 22 2026). It is the app's own icon on iPhone and iPad (light, dark and tinted home
  screens), the Safari for Mac app, and the Chrome and Safari toolbar button (light and dark). At
  toolbar sizes and the Mac's 16/32 px sizes it sits straight with thicker lines so it stays readable.
- The Pantry Pop logo beside the name in the dashboard sidebar and the toolbar popup shows the same
  binder. The other styles keep their bookmark logo.
- In the icon pickers (Chrome Settings → Appearance, iOS Appearance, the Mac app) the default icon is
  now called "Internet Binder". The Field Notebook, Gallery, Night Atlas and Swiss Index icons are unchanged.

## What did not change (so nothing is lost)

- Your library, settings and AI key: storage names, the IndexedDB database and the iOS App Group stay
  the same, so updating keeps everything.
- Store identity: the Chrome extension id, the bundle ids (com.harrisonfischberg.keepsake…), the
  keepsake:// link scheme and the export format marker (`keepsake-export`, so old exports still
  import). Xcode project, scheme and target names and the repo folder names are unchanged too.
- The privacy policy stays at rarecreatures.app/apps/keepsake/privacy/ until the site moves.

## Other

- Home's title row stacks the buttons under the name at the largest accessibility text sizes.
