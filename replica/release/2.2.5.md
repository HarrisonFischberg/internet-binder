# Internet Binder 2.2.5

The Chrome release of everything merged since 2.2.4 (PRs #51 to #72). iPhone, iPad and Mac Safari already
have these as TestFlight builds 9 to 25, which kept the 2.2.4 version number; the next upload (build 26) is 2.2.5.

## Everywhere (shared code)

- **New look.** Three appearances from the brand kit, Nocturne, Paper & Ink and Chromatic, replace all the
  old styles. The light/dark switch is gone (each appearance carries its own). Icons are unchanged.
- **Sorting learns.** Every collection you choose teaches the sorter, it catches up on the library you
  already filed, and your own keywords beat a collection you don't have. Settings shows the words it learned
  for each collection.

## Chrome

- The new look on the dashboard, popup, toast and floating button. Nothing else changes for Chrome: iCloud
  sync is Safari and iPhone only, and Chrome stays local-only.

## iPhone, iPad and Mac Safari (already on TestFlight)

- iCloud sync (iPhone and Safari on the Mac), with a progress screen and Work in background.
- Liquid Glass controls, picture cards, faster lists and taps.
- Share sheet Done closes at once with a firm two-beat buzz, and finishes the save after.
