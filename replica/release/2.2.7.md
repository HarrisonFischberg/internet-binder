# Internet Binder 2.2.7

The Chrome release of everything merged since 2.2.6 (PRs #86 to #95). iPhone, iPad and Mac Safari
already have these as TestFlight builds 33 to 36, which kept the 2.2.6 version number; the next upload
(build 37) is 2.2.7.

## Everywhere (shared code)

- **Sort every view.** Each collection and Lifestyle view has a Sort menu (Newest first, Oldest, A–Z, price),
  remembered per view.
- **Smarter AI filing.** AI sorting shows its evidence, labels and learning fixes from the overnight QA,
  and a place check before a save is filed as a place.
- **Places: Find on the map.** An AI button that pins your saved places on the map.
- **Import keeps your AI setup.** Replacing everything from a backup no longer resets the AI endpoint.

## Chrome

- All of the above. Chrome stays local-only; iCloud sync and Apple Intelligence are Apple-only.

## iPhone, iPad and Mac Safari (already on TestFlight)

- The Places map no longer freezes until iOS closes the app; Sort menus on the Collections and Lifestyle pages.
