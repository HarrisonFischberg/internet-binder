# Keepsake 2.1.1

Everything in 2.1.0 (see 2.1.0.md; its store review was cancelled before it went live), plus:

## New

- **Each recipe step shows the ingredients it uses.** In the recipe view, every step in the Method lists
  its ingredients with their amounts, ready to check off. A box checked there is checked in the
  Ingredients list too. Things made in an earlier step (the egg mix, the cooked pasta) show as
  "From step N", and ingredients no step names wait on step 1. Each style draws them its own way
  (Field Notebook also highlights the ingredient words in the step). The matching is plain word
  matching in keepsake/src/shared/stepIngredients.js, the same rules as the iPhone app.

## iPhone (same release, not on the App Store)

- Start cooking shows each step's ingredients inline, drawn per style, with a tappable progress bar
  (every step and the whole list) in place of the list button.
- "From step N" chips are labels now; tapping one no longer jumps back to that step.
- Amounts in brackets ("1 cup (150 g) fine cornmeal") stay with the amount, not the name.
