# Internet Binder 2.2.8

The Chrome release of everything merged since 2.2.7 (PRs #97 to #109). iPhone, iPad and Mac Safari
already have these as TestFlight builds 37 to 43; 2.2.8 build 43 is in App Review.

## Everywhere (shared code)

- **Link reader.** Saved links from TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, Bluesky, Pinterest, Threads, Facebook,
  Vimeo, Spotify, SoundCloud and any other page are read for a real title, picture and description.
- **X posts.** Posts from X save like Instagram posts, and the AI reads them.
- **Recipe from a link.** Paste a recipe's address on a thin recipe save to fill it in, with Undo.
- **Prefer my Claude key.** New saves can go to your own AI provider.

## Chrome

- All of the above. Chrome stays local-only; iCloud sync and Apple Intelligence are Apple-only.

## iPhone, iPad and Mac Safari (already on TestFlight / in review)

- Home's last card clears the tab bar; the Nocturne tagline reads "Organize your mind".
