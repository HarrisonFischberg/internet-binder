# Keepsake 2.1.3

Everything in 2.1.2 (see 2.1.2.md; it was built for Safari and iPhone but never published to the
Chrome Web Store, so the store goes from 2.1.1 to 2.1.3), plus:

## New

- **Refresh, and ⌘R / Ctrl+R, on the dashboard.** A Refresh button re-reads your library and redraws
  the page in place (useful in Safari on the Mac, where the dashboard has no reload button of its own).
  ⇧⌘R still reloads the whole page.
- **Lifestyle views and collections sort by most saves.** After a big import the sidebar, Home and All
  collections list the fullest first (ties A–Z), so the counts read top to bottom. All collections
  defaults to "Most saves". On iPhone, pinned collections stay first, and a manual drag order can be
  reset with "Sort by most saves".

## From 2.1.2

- Bulk imports (open tabs, Instagram) no longer send anything to your AI provider by themselves; Run AI
  on imported saves, when you ask, with progress, Pause, Resume and Cancel, and slow-down handling.
