# Keepsake 1.4.2

Upload after 1.4.1 has finished review (the store takes no new package while one is pending).

## What's new

- Instagram import goes into Lifestyle views, not only Collections. Every "where it goes" menu (each post,
  and *Put all selected in…*) lists Lifestyle and Collections.
- A Saved collection named for a Lifestyle view goes there: "Restaurants", "NYC eats", "Coffee shops" →
  Places; "Recipes" → Recipes; "Workouts" → Workouts. Otherwise a caption with a recipe, a workout or a 📍
  place goes to that view.
- Places from Instagram are named from the caption's 📍 line or the post's location tag, and keep the
  tag's street, city and map pin when Instagram has them, so they show on the Places map right away. A tag
  that is only a city ("Brooklyn, New York") is the area, not the name.
- After the import, Lookups and Optional AI (if on) fill in what's missing: an address and pin from
  OpenStreetMap, or the name and city from your AI provider (which now also sees the location tag).
- Moving an already-imported post into Places does the same.
- Fixed: importing posts with no collection (Review, or a Lifestyle view) stopped with "Data provided to
  an operation does not meet requirements."

- Places are listed by state (or country outside the US), then city. The state is read from the state
  field, the ZIP code, the end of the street address ("…2nd floor NY"), the city ("Brooklyn"), or the
  post ("in Carteret, NJ", "#nycfood").
- **Places review**, next to List and Map: places with no name of their own (a "Top 12…" post, a caption
  with only an address) or no state wait there instead of "Somewhere else". *Fix with AI* asks your
  provider for each one's name, street, city and state (a list post stays put); without AI, *Read again*
  reads every Instagram place's caption again with the new rules, replacing what an older reading wrote
  (never what you typed).
- Caption reading: the ZIP is no longer part of the state; "(@handle) in City, ST"; "(Hollywood, Los
  Angeles)"; a "Location :" label, "This spot is closed" or "3 Locations" is not a name; suites and floors
  stay in the street; an address with no 📍; "Follow @…" and photo credits are not the place; three or
  more 📍 is a list.
- Lookups store the city as the city and the state as its code (they used to store a neighbourhood as the
  city and the city as the state).
- Background checks show progress: a bar under the header on every page ("Fixing places with AI… 12 of 52"),
  with Stop, then what changed ("31 places moved to your list").
- **Check with AI** moved out of Settings to where your saves are: next to the title of every page that
  lists saves (Library, Favorites, Review, each collection: titles, prices, collections, shopping saves
  only; each Lifestyle view: fills in what's missing). On the Places list or map it checks every place,
  filling a missing street, city or state and never replacing what's there; in Places review, the places
  waiting there. Settings keeps a pointer and the change log.
- Fixed: saving a setting (the AI switch and its confirmation, Save on Optional AI…) jumped the page
  back to the top. Pages now keep their place when they redraw, everywhere in the dashboard; and Settings
  no longer scrolls the whole window as well as its content.
- Fixed: "null" showed in the import's details banner and in a place's Edit details.

## Data

- New optional field `instagram.place` on a save (the location tag's name, street, city, region,
  postcode, pin). Older builds drop it when they rewrite a save; nothing else changes, nothing to migrate.
