# Keepsake 1.4.2

Upload after 1.4.1 has finished review (the store takes no new package while one is pending).

## What's new

- Instagram import goes into Lifestyle views, not only Collections. Every "where it goes" menu (each post,
  and *Put all selected in…*) lists Lifestyle and Collections.
- A Saved collection named for a Lifestyle view goes there: "Restaurants", "NYC eats", "Coffee shops" →
  Places; "Recipes" → Recipes; "Workouts" → Workouts. Otherwise a caption with a recipe, a workout or a 📍
  place goes to that view.
- Places from Instagram are named from the caption's 📍 line or the post's location tag, and keep the
  tag's street, city and map pin when Instagram has them, so they show on the Places map right away. A tag
  that is only a city ("Brooklyn, New York") is the area, not the name.
- After the import, Lookups and Optional AI (if on) fill in what's missing: an address and pin from
  OpenStreetMap, or the name and city from your AI provider (which now also sees the location tag).
- Moving an already-imported post into Places does the same.
- Fixed: importing posts with no collection (Review, or a Lifestyle view) stopped with "Data provided to
  an operation does not meet requirements."

## Data

- New optional field `instagram.place` on a save (the location tag's name, street, city, region,
  postcode, pin). Older builds drop it when they rewrite a save; nothing else changes, nothing to migrate.
