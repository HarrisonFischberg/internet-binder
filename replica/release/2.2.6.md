# Internet Binder 2.2.6

The Chrome release of everything merged since 2.2.5 (PRs #65 and #71 to #84). iPhone, iPad and Mac Safari
already have these as TestFlight builds 26 to 32, which kept the 2.2.5 version number; the next upload
(build 33) is 2.2.6.

## Everywhere (shared code)

- **Security: MapLibre GL 6.4.1** for the Places map, fixing GHSA-jrc7-96c5-q579 (an XSS in the map's
  attribution sanitizer). The map now loads as an ES module with its worker from the extension itself.
- **Web recipes.** A recipe post that never wrote its recipe down gets a close one from the web, labelled
  "Based on a recipe from <site>".
- **Cooking music.** Ten real, freely licensed songs at 20% volume, Tap to unmute, credits in Settings.
- **Faster AI reads.** The understand prompt is cached, so bulk AI reads reuse it.
- **Speed.** Fewer redraws and lighter search; Places and the workout and recipe views are their own modules.

## Chrome

- All of the above. Chrome stays local-only; iCloud sync and Apple Intelligence are Apple-only.

## iPhone, iPad and Mac Safari (already on TestFlight)

- Apple Intelligence reads saves on the device; iCloud sync fixes; the Mac app's Open Safari Settings button
  and On/Off status now work (it asked Safari about the wrong extension ID).
