# Keepsake 2.1.2

Everything in 2.1.1 (see 2.1.1.md), plus:

## Changed

- **Bulk imports no longer send anything to your AI provider by themselves.** Import from tabs and
  Instagram imports (one collection or all of them) are sorted on your device only, and each save is
  flagged as not read by AI yet. A big import (1,000+ posts) used to fire one AI request per save at
  once and stall.
- **Run AI on imported saves**, when you ask: Home shows "N imported saves haven't been read by AI",
  and a collection or Lifestyle view shows the same for its own saves. It asks first, with the model,
  the count and a time estimate, and offers the newest 100 or all of them. It runs in the background,
  two at a time, with progress under the header and Pause, Resume and Cancel. When your provider asks
  to slow down (429, overloaded, timeouts) it waits and tries again; a bad key or no credit pauses it
  and says why. Saves not read yet stay waiting, so a run resumes after Chrome restarts.
- On the first start after updating, saves left over from an earlier import that were still waiting
  for the AI tidy, and Instagram imports your provider never read, move into this queue instead of
  being sent all at once.

## iPhone (same release, not on the App Store)

- Imported backups (AirDropped from Chrome) wait for Run AI too. Home and Settings → Optional AI show
  "Run AI on N imported saves", with the same slow-down handling and Pause.
