# Keepsake 1.4.0

Release notes, and what to change by hand on the Chrome Web Store listing. The release workflow uploads
and publishes the package; it does not touch the listing text.

## What's new (for the listing and the GitHub release)

Keepsake now saves more than shopping. Products still sort themselves into Collections. Everything else
goes into its own **Lifestyle** view:

- **Places**: restaurants, cafés, salons, gyms, shops. Keepsake reads the address, hours and phone from
  the page, an Instagram caption ("📍 …"), a maps link, or a business's Instagram profile or Facebook
  page. A **Map** shows them all. Optional OpenStreetMap lookups fill in a missing address and pin.
- **Recipes**: ingredients, steps, servings and time, in a recipe view with a checklist.
- **Workouts**: exercises with sets, reps and rest. Make a goal ("3× a week for 4 weeks") and check in.
- **Videos** (anything from YouTube, and other video sites), **articles**, **films & shows** and
  **books**, with optional Wikidata and Open Library lookups for the details.
- Save anything anywhere: every "where does this go?" menu lists the Lifestyle views and your
  Collections.
- A new dashboard: Home with *Come back to*, a Library with filters, search that fixes typos, tags,
  grid or list, and undo (Alt+Z).
- Your library moves to IndexedDB, so saves survive anything Chrome does to extension storage. The old
  copy is left untouched, so going back to 1.3 still works.

Still: no account, no server, no telemetry. Lookups and AI are off until you turn them on, and each asks
for only the one site it needs.

## Short description (manifest.json, 124 of 132 characters)

> Save products, places, recipes, workouts, videos and more from any site. Sorted for you and kept on your device. No account.

## Change by hand in the Developer Dashboard (before or right after publishing)

- **Detailed description**: replace "universal wishlist" wording with the text above.
- **Single purpose**: "Save things you find on the web (products, places, recipes, workouts, videos,
  articles, films and books) and keep them organized on your device."
- **Permission justifications**: no new permissions in `manifest.json`. The lookups request
  `nominatim.openstreetmap.org`, `tiles.openfreemap.org`, `www.wikidata.org` and `openlibrary.org` at run
  time, through the existing optional host permission (`*://*/*`); describe them under that permission.
- **Privacy practices**: still no data collected by the developer. Lookups send a place's name and
  city, or a title, to those public services, only when you turn them on (see `PRIVACY.md`).
- **Privacy policy**: paste the updated `keepsake/PRIVACY.md`.
- **Screenshots**: the current ones show only shopping. Add a Places map, a recipe and the Library.
