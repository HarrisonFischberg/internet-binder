# Keepsake 1.4.5

## What's new

- **Import all your Instagram Saved collections at once.** On your Saved page
  (instagram.com/<you>/saved/), the popup offers **Import all collections**. The tab opens each
  collection in turn, scrolls it to the end, then "All posts" for anything in no collection. One review
  lists every collection with its count; by default each Instagram collection becomes a group with the
  same name (a checkbox lets Keepsake sort them instead). Recipes, workouts and places still go to their
  Lifestyle views. Stop any time and keep what was found. Limits: 2,000 posts per collection, 5,000 in all;
  a post in two collections goes to the first.
- After the import, Settings → Export JSON makes a file you can AirDrop to Keepsake on iPhone (the iOS app
  opens it directly once it ships with this change).

## Privacy

- `PRIVACY.md` describes Import all collections: it reads the collection list on your Saved page, opens
  each collection in that tab, and needs the optional `https://www.instagram.com/*` permission. The
  copy at https://rarecreatures.app/apps/keepsake/privacy/ needs the same update.

## Data

- Scan posts carry an optional `collections` list (names of the Instagram collections a post was in);
  the session summary carries `bulk` progress. Nothing to migrate.
