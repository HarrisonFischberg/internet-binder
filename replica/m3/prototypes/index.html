Keepsake for iPhone: three directions

Static SwiftUI prototypes rendered on the iOS 27 Simulator (iPhone 17). Gradients stand in for real photos. Pick one direction (or a mix) at the M3.0 review.

Postcard

Paper things on a warm table. Each save is the object of its kind: a postcard, a recipe card, a ticket stub, a book, a swing tag. New York serif for names, ink-blue for actions.

Cabinet

A collector's drawer. Every save rests in a recessed tray with a numbered specimen label; index tabs filter; a brass pull opens search. Condensed sans and mono labels.

Daylight

Photos first, under glass. Big rounded images, Liquid Glass controls floating over content, rounded type, cobalt actions. The closest to current iOS.
