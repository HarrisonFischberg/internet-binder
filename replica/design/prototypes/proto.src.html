Keepsake design review

A proposed design system for Keepsake on Chrome, iPhone, iPad and Mac, in three styles people can choose between. Every style shares the same layouts, parts and words, and each has light and dark versions.

  • Modern (default): crisp cool greys, a cobalt accent, bold sans headings, soft depth.
  • Moss: Keepsake's original moss green and brass, serif headings, warm neutrals.
  • Minimalist: black and white, hairline edges instead of shadows, quiet colour on the kinds.
Static visual prototype. These are drawings in a web page with sample content, not the Chrome extension or a native app. Nothing here saves data, and the iPhone and Mac frames are not SwiftUI.

07 · For your decision

What I'd like you to approve

Three styles

Modern by default, with Moss and Minimalist in Settings → Appearance next to System, Light and Dark. Same layouts and parts in all three.

First slice to build

Chrome capture card (popup and in-page) and Review, on the save-first storage from M1. The rest of the dashboard stays as it is until M2.

Open choices

Decided: grid by default, favourites stay a star. Still open: which style new users start with (Modern is proposed).
