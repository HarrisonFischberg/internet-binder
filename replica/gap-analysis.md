# Gap analysis: Keepsake against Thingz and Albo

Date: 2026-10-11. Evidence keys: review ids are in `replica/reviews.csv`; T## and A## are screens in `replica/thingz/recon.md` and `replica/albo/recon.md`; A1/V2 and similar are findings in `replica/keepsake-audit.md`.

- **Confidence** says how sure we are that the improvement matters to users: high (several independent signals), medium (one competitor or a few reviews), low (hypothesis or owner direction only).
- **Cost** is relative: S (days), M (1–2 weeks), L (several weeks), XL (a quarter).
- **Privacy impact** is what changes about data leaving the device.

## Explicit exclusions (with reason)

| excluded | reason |
| --- | --- |
| Friends feed, comments, likes, follows, public curated collections, community chat, blends | Their network; against private-by-default; little review demand |
| Hosted AI, "ask your library" chat as a core feature | Needs a server or always-on AI. Only reconsider as BYOK and opt-in |
| Meal planner, grocery list, film/book trackers, game tracking (workouts moved into scope 2026-10-11) | Albo's breadth is XL and its reliability complaints cluster around it. Revisit after kinds ship |
| MCP connector | Hosted OAuth in Albo. A local-only variant could be revisited later |
| Weekly limits, paywalls | Against the product promise |
| Logging in to Instagram/TikTok on the user's behalf | Least privilege; the iOS plan decision |
| Android | Owner decision |
