My pick: Option 2

The Home card meets people when they open Keepsake and wonder how to start, it stays until the Share sheet actually delivers a save, and the Settings row keeps the how-to one tap away for anyone who dismissed it. Option 1 is the easiest to skip and gone for good afterwards. Option 3 has the best timing, but it only reaches people who already found Keepsake, and they can't follow the steps from inside the save card.

All three share the same how-to sheet and animation, so Option 3's tip can be added later for very little.

Which one should I build?

Mockups only, drawn in HTML from Keepsake's real style tokens and shapes (Theme.swift and StyleTokens.swift). The Share sheet in the animation is iOS's own, so it keeps system colours. Try it now relies on Keepsake showing up in its own Share sheet, which needs a quick check on a real iPhone before building.
